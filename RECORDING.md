Use the shot list and narration in DEMO.md. The video should show the corrected public report pages or their local static copy. Identify the footage as an archived-report walkthrough, not a fresh live run.

Capture the actual interface at a readable desktop size. Keep the cursor and transitions restrained. Match every spoken number and quotation to the visible report.

The local audio file `runs/demoaudio_05_pii.wav` belongs to the later Repeat sample. It is not audio of the first baseline finding. If used, identify that source and its timestamp on screen. The Baseline report must not play a different run's audio under its transcript.

Before delivery, watch the entire MP4 with sound. Check that captions agree with the narration, no private browser tabs or credentials appear, and the final URL is readable. An exported file is not a completed platform submission.
